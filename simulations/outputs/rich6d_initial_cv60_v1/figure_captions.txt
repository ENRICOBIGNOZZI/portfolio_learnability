comparison_baseline
Baseline: N=600, rho=0.95. Means and central 95% replication-percentile bands across 300 paths. CV selects a once within the first 60 observations and freezes it thereafter. The oracle is an ex-post population diagnostic. Horizontal lines distinguish the economic optimum from the frozen Nyström subspace optimum. Grey areas retain original checked numerical failures; crosses identify CV mean-regret floor failures. All CV policies lack a new higher-rank certificate.
