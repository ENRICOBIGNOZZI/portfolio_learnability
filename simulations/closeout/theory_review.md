The Matérn multiplier on R^6 has order -9; zero extension of an L2 input and convolution maps to H^9 before restriction to the cube. Together with H_1=H^(9/2) and interpolation of the operator-range scale, this proves the necessary embedding H_r into H^((9/2)r). It does not prove the converse on a bounded nonperiodic domain. For the ideal infinite series, the H^(9/2) squared coefficient sum is proportional to sum 1/(m log^2 m), whereas every higher Sobolev sum diverges. Absolute third-derivative convergence gives C^3; Dirichlet summation gives an unbounded fourth derivative at a period seam. The nonzero coefficient of that singular derivative occurs on an open positive-measure set of interior slices. Fubini/Plancherel for an extension to R^6 and one-dimensional Sobolev embedding rule out H^(9/2+delta) for every delta>0. Sobolev algebra and smooth composition give membership at 9/2.
