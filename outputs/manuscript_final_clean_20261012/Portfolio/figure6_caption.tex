\caption{Historical out-of-sample Sharpe--complexity profiles.
Each panel reports the realized out-of-sample Sharpe ratio associated with
the complete ridge regularization path of the fixed-lengthscale Gaussian
representation. The nine panels cover eight five-year decision blocks and
one 2018--2024 block. For each candidate penalty, monthly out-of-sample
returns are concatenated across the annual refits in the block, and a single
Sharpe ratio is computed. Effective complexity is averaged across those
refits, weighted by the number of evaluated months. Curves connect the actual
evaluated penalties, including the extended positive grid and the exact
minimum-norm zero-penalty endpoint, without smoothing or extrapolation.
Stars indicate retrospective test-sample maxima; open circles indicate the
unregularized endpoints. The dynamically selected annual cross-validation
strategy is not plotted as a point on these curves because its penalty may
differ across annual refits. All panels use common axes. The historical
maxima are descriptive and are not estimates of population-optimal complexity.}
\label{fig:complexity_nine}
