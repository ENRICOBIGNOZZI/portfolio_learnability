# From regression drift to direct financial decisions

The adaptive-memory paper models independent pairs (X_s,Y_s), a fixed covariate law and a drifting f*(u,x)=E[Y|X=x]. Its weighted neural risk theorem includes approximation, estimation, drift and optimization terms. JKP observations are market-month panels with cross-sectional and temporal dependence. They do not satisfy those assumptions merely by changing a target name.

Here Y=1 is artificial and constant. The payoff predictor is g_theta(Z,R)=sum_i f_theta(Z_i)R_i/N_t. Characteristics alone determine positions. Returns appear only after decision in loss and scoring. E[Y|Z,R] remains 1. Potential drift is in the panel/payoff law P_t, risk Q_t(theta)=E_Pt[(1-g_theta)^2], and optimal policy. Establishing a theorem would require a quantified risk/policy displacement bound, stability and identification of payoff geometry, a suitable dependence/concentration treatment, neural approximation/complexity control, and optimization guarantees. No financial minimax rate or new mixing theorem is claimed here.

The simplex surrogate minimizes ||omega||^2+tau(d'omega)^2. Tau is a drift-to-estimation tradeoff for temporal weights, not a network penalty. Its strictly convex active-set solution is [A-Bd]_+. Tau=0 returns exact uniform weights. For B>0, H=B_t*(A/B)^(1/gamma) produces identical normalized empirical taper weights, including H larger than observed history. Both theory and empirical families share that functional form; differences in independently specified grids or search budgets are not evidence for a superior theorem. Matched weights and deterministic optimizer parity are tested independently.

For any payoff direction, training moments mu=sum omega*p and M2=sum omega*p^2 give the exact optimal scale c*=mu/M2. The minimum along that ray is 1-mu^2/M2=1/(1+SR_omega^2), when variance is positive. The NN's final affine layer permits free scaling without declaring the nonlinear neural class a linear space. The final training-only scalar is incorporated into its output. Adam's finite computation does not certify a global optimum or OOS Sharpe improvement; calibration only optimizes scale along the learned direction.

The final portfolio PDF's stationary RKHS estimator, its attainability assumptions and its proofs are distinct from the deep no-penalty NN. The earlier portfolio version supports the operational N_t normalization, not an automatic extension of all fixed-N proofs to an unbalanced panel. No old table or Sharpe is relabeled as a result of this new estimator.

The monthly simultaneous bootstrap gate is a prespecified empirical guard. Its common circular blocks retain pairing, and its max statistic adjusts for candidates in that family. It is not finite-sample exact under drift, not a confidence sequence, not a stationarity test, and does not guarantee zero stationary loss. Sequential reuse and detection delay remain costs. Synthetic stationary/break controls measure those costs separately from retrospective market evidence.
